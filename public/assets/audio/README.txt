The current application plays no background music or speech.
All live sounds are short Web Audio interaction effects defined in src/audio/AudioManager.js.
The three WAV files here are unused historical compositions, retained for reference; none is requested by the application.
The previous music generator is historical and is not part of startup or runtime audio.
Effects begin only after user interaction, support mute/volume, and stop on pause/reset. There are no idle loops.
